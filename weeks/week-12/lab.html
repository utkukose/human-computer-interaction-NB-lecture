3D interfaces lab

Three parts of this interactive lab are games in three dimensions (3D). Part A lets you point a ray at the signs of the atrium in the virtual tour. Part C takes you through the virtual hospital, steering and teleporting, and Part D guides you through the corridors with glasses of augmented reality. Parts B and D compute the formulas of the lecture step by step, and Part E asks for the scores of the Simulator Sickness Questionnaire by hand. In the calculators every intermediate value is rounded, and the next step continues with the rounded value. The results of Parts A, C and D can be copied into the Colab notebook. Each game first shows its place in the hospital. Start moves the view to the game, and the view returns to the room at the end. The games work best with a mouse or a touchpad on a computer.

Part A: Signs at a distance

In the virtual tour, a patient stands in the atrium of the hospital and points a ray from the controller in the hand at the signs of the departments. Twelve signs appear one at a time, from 1.5 to 9 m away and from 0.15 to 0.6 m wide. The ray trembles by 0.8 degrees in each direction, as the hand of a patient would.

In round 1, select each sign with the plain ray: Move the mouse until the ray lies on the sign and click. The first click on each sign counts, hit or miss. In round 2, the ray snaps to a sign when it passes within 2 degrees of its centre, and the sign lights up. The view stays fixed during a round, as if the patient held the head still.

Part B: The ray and the hand step by step

The first calculator repeats the worked examples of the ray one step at a time. Its sliders set the distance and the width of a sign, the scatter of the ray and the snap zone. The figure shows the sign and the snap zone in degrees, with the scatter of the ray.

Part C: Through the virtual hospital

The virtual tour leads from the lift on the ground floor to room 12. The corridor runs ahead for 10 m, turns right for 6 m and turns left again for 8 m to the door of room 12. You take the tour twice: In round 1 you steer, and in round 2 you teleport.

Steering: Hold W or ↑ to glide forward at 1.4 m/s, and A and D or ← and → to turn at 60 degrees per second. Teleporting: Point at the floor within 4 m and click to jump there, and turn in steps of 45 degrees with Q and E. The buttons below the view do the same on a touch screen. At the door of room 12, turn the view and click in the direction of the lift where you started.

Part D: Arrows in the corridor

On the day of the visit, the clinic app guides a patient from the main entrance to cardiology through glasses of augmented reality. The route has six turns. At each turn, the walk stops a few metres before the junction, and a cyan arrow on the floor shows where to turn. Doors to other rooms lie before and after each turn, and a sign hangs above the junction, as in a real hospital.

Click the opening that you would take: The turn into the side corridor or one of the doors. In round 1, the glasses track their own motion without anchors, so the arrow drifts with the metres walked. In round 2, a marker at each junction anchors the arrow. The walk moves on after each choice, right or wrong.

Part E: By hand: The sickness score of the tour

After the steered tour, a patient rates eyestrain as moderate (2), and general discomfort, fatigue, difficulty focusing, nausea, fullness of head, blurred vision and stomach awareness as slight (1). Every other symptom is rated none (0). Use the table of the lecture to add up the raw sums, and compute the three scores and the total score of the Simulator Sickness Questionnaire. Give each score to two decimals.
