Visual design and emotion lab

Three parts of this interactive lab are experiments in three dimensions (3D) that record your own perception and feelings. Part A flashes dot patterns on the floor of the waiting area. Part C flashes screen designs in the design studio, and Part D lets you book at the kiosk with waits in different orders. Parts B and D compute the formulas of the lecture step by step, and Part E asks for the values of stage 8 by hand. In the calculators every intermediate value is rounded, and the next step continues with the rounded value. The results of Parts A, C and D can be copied into the Colab notebook. Each game first shows its room of the hospital. Start moves the view to the game, and the view returns to the room at the end. The games work best with a mouse or a touchpad on a computer.

Part A: Floor dots in the waiting area

The clinic plans floor markings for its new waiting area, and the designers want to know which rows patients see in a pattern of dots. In the game, a projector on the ceiling flashes a pattern of dots onto a dark mat on the floor for 300 ms, followed by a mask of scattered dots. The view looks straight down from the ceiling.

After each pattern, choose the direction of the rows that you saw from the four circles below the view, or press the keys 1 to 4. There is no right answer: Report what you saw at first glance. The thirty patterns are rectangular lattices with the ratios 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 and 1.4 between the longer and the shorter distance, six of each, at random orientations.

Part C: Fifty milliseconds in the design studio

The design team tests twelve designs for the start screen of the kiosk on the large screen of the studio. In round 1, a cross appears first, then a design for only 50 ms, then a mask of coloured blocks. Rate how appealing the design looked with the buttons from 1 to 9 below the view, or with the number keys.

In round 2, the same designs return in a new order, and each one stays on the screen until you rate it. The designs differ in their number of elements, their colours and their alignment, as in Section 3 of the Colab notebook. The page records how long each flash really stayed on the screen, which depends on the refresh rate of the display.

Part D: Waits at the kiosk

The game takes place at the kiosk in the lobby, with other patients waiting for their turn. Book eight appointments, five taps each: A department, a doctor, a day, a time and the confirmation. After every tap, the kiosk waits before it shows the next step.

Every booking waits 6 s in total, but in one of four orders. The long wait comes at the end, at the start or in the middle, or the waits spread evenly. The page does not tell you which order comes next. After each booking, rate how it felt with the buttons from 1 to 9 below the view, or with the number keys.

Part E: By hand: The waits of stage 8

The current app waits 0.4, 0.4, 0.5, 0.4 and 3.0 s after the five steps of a booking. The redesign waits 3.0, 0.4, 0.4, 0.5 and 0.4 s. Compute the mean wait per step to two decimals. Then compute the peak-end value of the current waits and that of the redesign.
