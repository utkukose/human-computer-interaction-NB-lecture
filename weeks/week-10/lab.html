Accessibility lab

Three parts of this interactive lab are games in three dimensions (3D). Part A sets up the kiosk in the main hall for ten patients. Part C lets you rewrite two screens of the kiosk, and Part D puts you at the kiosk with a screen reader. Parts B and D compute the formulas of the lecture step by step, and Part E asks for the Ateşman score of stage 10 by hand. In the calculators every intermediate value is rounded, and the next step continues with the rounded value. The results of Parts A, C and D can be copied into the Colab notebook. Each game first shows its room of the hospital. Start moves the view to the game, and the view returns to the room at the end. The games work best with a mouse or a touchpad on a computer.

Part A: The kiosk for everyone

The kiosk in the main hall of the outpatient clinics was installed for standing patients. The centre of its screen is at 1.42 m, the screen is upright, and its body reaches the floor. Ten patients of the clinic come to book at it. Each one reaches for the top and the bottom row of buttons, and a line shows the angle at which the patient sees the screen. The board behind them measures the heights.

In round 1, the patients try the kiosk as installed: Call them one by one with Next patient or the key N. Then set the height of the screen centre, its tilt backwards and the knee space with the controls below the view, and start round 2 with the same patients. A patient is served when both rows are within reach and the screen is seen at no more than 30°.

Part C: Plain words at the kiosk

The kiosk shows two screens of text: The confirmation after a booking and the message when a patient number is not found. Each screen has four sentences, and each sentence has three versions, from formal to plain. Choose a version for each sentence with the buttons below the view, and the kiosk shows your text with its reading level at once.

When the text is ready, press Show it to the patients. Twelve patients with reading levels from grade 2 to grade 14 read it one after another. A patient understands the text when its Flesch-Kincaid grade is not above the patient's level and the text keeps all four facts. Watch the shortest versions: One of them drops a fact.

Part D: Listening to the kiosk

A blind patient books at the kiosk with headphones and the tactile keypad below the screen. You take her place: The screen stays dark, and the kiosk speaks only through its screen reader, whose words appear in the caption below the view. The task: An appointment in cardiology on Wednesday at 10:30, then confirm it.

Use the keypad below the view or your keyboard: ↓ next element, ↑ previous element, H next heading, Enter activate and R repeat. Round 1 runs the current app and round 2 the app of stage 10. Every press counts, and activating a wrong button counts as an error.

Part E: By hand: The Turkish confirmation

The plain Turkish confirmation of stage 10 reads: Randevunuz alındı. Lütfen on beş dakika erken gelin ve sağlık kartınızı getirin. Gelemezseniz bir gün önce iptal edin. It has 18 words, 3 sentences and 41 syllables, one for every vowel. Compute the syllables per word, the words per sentence and the Ateşman score. Round each intermediate value to three decimals, as in the lecture.
