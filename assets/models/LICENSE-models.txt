Human figures of the 3D scenes (man.js, woman.js, oldman.js, oldwoman.js)

The figures were built from the assets of the MakeHuman project (https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman):
the base mesh hm08, the macro targets for gender, age, muscle,
weight and ethnicity, the default skeleton with its weights, and the helper geometry for tights, skirt and eyes. These
assets were released by their copyright holders under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal dedication.

The figures themselves are also dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Each file holds one glTF 2.0 binary model, encoded in Base64, so that the pages can load it from the disk.
